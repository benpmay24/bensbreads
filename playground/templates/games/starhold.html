How to play Starhold

Goal

Find at least 2 of 3 hidden enemy stars, or eliminate every enemy piece.

Setup

Each player drafts any mix of 12 pieces, then secretly places 3 stars on their half of the 10×8 board (West vs East).

Turns

On your turn, either select a piece already on the board to command it, or tap Deploy to place one reserve piece onto your back line.

Pieces

  • Circle — move 1 · destroy walls · beats triangle & square
  • Triangle — move 2 · only beats triangle
  • Square — move 1 · build/destroy up to 2 walls
  • Trapezoid — move 1 · beats circle, triangle & square

Same-type clashes: the attacker always wins. Failed attacks destroy the attacker.

Walls

Walls sit on tile edges and block movement until destroyed. Circles may destroy 1 adjacent wall per turn; squares may build or destroy up to 2. Destroying a wall uses a wall action. Marked coral edges show walls you can break.
